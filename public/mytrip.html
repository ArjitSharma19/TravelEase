Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

Welcome to TravelEase. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting the personal data of first-time Indian travellers going abroad.

1. Information We Collect: We collect personal details you provide directly, such as name, email address, passport expiry date, trip preferences, and travel destinations when you register, search flights, or subscribe to newsletters.

2. How We Use Your Information: We use your details to calculate visa warnings, provide live exchange alerts, customize travel checklists, and deliver email updates about visa changes.

3. Data Security: Your data is stored securely in our MongoDB database and is hashed or protected using modern encryption standards. We never sell or share your data with third parties.
