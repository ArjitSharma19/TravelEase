Smart Travel Tips for First-Timers

Essential advice from experienced Indian travellers

View All Tips →
Visa

Apply for visa early

Start paperwork weeks ahead so appointments, documents, and approvals do not delay your trip.

Money

Use forex card

Carry a low-fee travel card like Niyo or Wise and keep only small emergency cash.

SIM

Buy eSIM before travel

Install your eSIM before flying so maps, cabs, and hotel calls work as soon as you land.

Documents

Always carry physical copies

Keep printed backups of your visa, passport, travel insurance, and bookings in case of device failure.

Health

Get travel insurance

Protect yourself from unexpected medical emergencies, trip cancellations, or lost baggage abroad.

Money

Notify your bank early

Inform your card issuer of your travel dates and destination to prevent security blocks on transactions.

Tech

Download offline maps

Save maps of your destination city offline to navigate streets and locate hotels without cellular data.

Luggage

Check baggage limits

Review airline weight limits for cabin and checked luggage to avoid hefty excess fees at the airport.
